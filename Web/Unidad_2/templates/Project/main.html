💡 Key Insight

"The best talent is NOT always where everyone is looking"

Geographic diversification reduces competition, lowers costs, and improves hiring success rates while maintaining quality standards.

Real-world data shows: Mexico (125 tech jobs) vs USA (4,850 jobs) — Same salary range ($138K vs $149K = 8% difference), but 97% less competition.

📌 Data Sources & Methodology

  • Salary Data: Real averages from 28,050 tech employment records (2020-2025)
  • Job Market Size: Actual tech job postings by country from dataset
  • Ad Costs ($6, $12, $20/click): Industry averages from LinkedIn/Google Ads by market
  • Acceptance Rates (50%, 70%): Typical patterns observed in saturated vs emerging markets

💡 Think of it like gas prices: The average price is real, but you can find it cheaper or more expensive depending on the station.
