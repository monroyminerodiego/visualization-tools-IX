Hierarchical Market Treemap
Interactive drill-down visualization showing Region → Country hierarchy. Size represents Total GDP (market capacity), while color intensity indicates GDP per capita (purchasing power). Click on any region to explore individual countries.
Population vs. GDP per Capita Analysis
Bubble chart revealing the relationship between workforce size (X-axis: Population) and purchasing power (Y-axis: GDP per capita). Bubble size represents Total GDP. Hover over bubbles to see detailed country metrics.
Regional Comparison Matrix
Comprehensive 4-metric dashboard comparing regions across key dimensions: Total GDP, Average GDP per Capita, Number of Countries, and Total Population. This multi-dimensional view helps prioritize regional strategies.
Market Share Sunburst
Hierarchical sunburst showing the proportional contribution of each market. Inner ring represents regions, outer ring shows individual countries. Click on segments to zoom in and explore market composition.
