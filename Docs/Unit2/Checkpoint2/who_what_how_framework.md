# Who-What-How Framework
## TCF Global Market Expansion Analysis

---

## WHO:

### Primary Decision-Maker
**Marco Antonelli** - CEO, Terra Cotta Foods (TCF)

**Profile Characteristics:**
- **Decision Style:** Fast, data-driven, ROI-focused
- **Time Constraint:** 3-minute attention window for strategic decisions
- **Data Literacy:** High operational/financial literacy; needs complex data distilled into business metrics
- **Current Challenge:** Planning ambitious expansion into Latin America and Asia
- **Core Priorities:**
  1. Supply chain efficiency and cost control
  2. Geopolitical risk mitigation
  3. Supplier diversification
  4. Distribution network optimization

**Pain Points:**
- Evaluating multiple markets simultaneously
- Balancing purchasing power vs. operational feasibility
- Understanding population-labor force dynamics
- Validating business hypotheses quickly

---

## WHAT:

### Critical Questions Marco Needs Answered:

1. **Market Viability:** Which regions justify investment in distribution centers?
2. **Purchase Power Hierarchy:** Where is the highest GDP concentration?
3. **Supply Risk Assessment:** How does population correlate with economic potential in target manufacturing locations?
4. **Product Strategy:** Should TCF focus on premium or mass-market products in each region?

### Expected Outcomes from Presentation:
- Clear go/no-go decision on continent-level expansion
- Top 5-10 country priorities ranked by strategic value
- Product portfolio adjustment recommendations
- Risk-adjusted investment roadmap

---

## HOW: 

### Core Data Framework
**3-Layer Analysis:**
1. **Regional Overview** → Strategic regional allocation
2. **Country Deep-Dive** → Tactical market entry priorities  
3. **Correlation Insights** → Product strategy validation

### Visualization Principles for Marco:

#### 1. Hierarchy First
- Use **TreeMaps** and **Sunburst Charts** to show nested relationships
- Region → Country → Economic metrics progression
- Size = Total GDP (purchasing power)
- Color = GDP per capita (premium market potential)

#### 2. Direct Data Labels
- Avoid cluttered axes; embed key numbers directly on visual elements
- Show absolute values ($B USD) and percentages simultaneously
- Highlight Top 10 countries with contrasting colors

#### 3. Horizontal Bar Charts
- For regional comparisons and country rankings
- Enable quick scanning and comparison
- Sort by descending value for instant prioritization

#### 4. Correlation Matrices → Business Translation
- Convert statistical correlations into strategic implications
- Population × Total GDP = **Consumer Base Strength**
- GDP per capita × Total GDP = **Premium Product Viability**
- Present as actionable segments: "High Volume/Low Margin" vs. "Low Volume/High Margin"

### Key Metrics to Emphasize:

**Primary Metrics:**
- Total GDP (B USD) → Market size & distribution scale potential
- GDP per capita (USD) → Product positioning & pricing power
- Population (M) → Labor force availability & consumer base

**Derived Metrics:**
- Market Concentration: Top 10 countries = 69.2% of global GDP
- Regional Balance: America vs. Asia (nearly equal at ~$38T vs. $37T)
- Economic Potential Score = Total GDP × 0.6 + (GDP per capita/1000) × 0.4

### Narrative Flow (3-Minute Structure):

**Minute 1: The Global Landscape (30 seconds)**
- 5 regions, 175 countries analyzed
- $107T global GDP, 7.7B population
- Market concentration insight: 69.2% in Top 10

**Minute 2: The Strategic Tri-Region Focus (90 seconds)**
- America, Asia, Europa = 96% of target GDP
- Show regional treemap with top countries highlighted
- Present correlation findings: Population = consumer base strength
- Key insight: Latin America & Asia offer best expansion balance

**Minute 3: The Action Plan (60 seconds)**
- Top 10 country priorities with 3-tier categorization:
  - **Tier 1:** Giant Markets (USA, China, Germany, Japan, India)
  - **Tier 2:** High Premium Potential (UK, France, Italy, Canada)
  - **Tier 3:** Volume Growth Plays (Brazil, Mexico, Indonesia)
- Product strategy: Mass-market focus for Asia/LatAm, premium for established Europa
- Next steps: Infrastructure assessment & geopolitical risk analysis

## Data Visualization Specifications

### Chart 1: Hierarchical Treemap
**Purpose:** Show GDP concentration with nested Global → Region → Country hierarchy  
**Key Feature:** 
- Rectangle size = Total GDP (market capacity)
- Color = Regional identification (consistent across all visualizations)
- Interactive drill-down (click regions to explore countries)
- Grouping strategy: Countries with GDP < $100B grouped as "Other [Region]"
**Labels:** Direct labels on rectangles with GDP values
**Design Rationale:** Matches Marco's hierarchical thinking: "Region first, then country details"

### Chart 2: Scatter Plot - Viability Matrix (Top 10 Countries Only)
**Purpose:** Validate population-GDP correlation for supply chain decisions  
**Key Feature:** 
- X-axis = Population (log scale) - workforce availability
- Y-axis = GDP per capita - purchasing power
- Bubble size = Total GDP - market size
- Top 10 markets only (reduces clutter, focuses on actionable targets)
- Country codes (3-letter) displayed on bubbles for quick identification
**Insight Callout:** "Strong correlation (0.80-0.98) validates workforce = opportunity hypothesis"
**Design Rationale:** Answers Marco's specific question about population-economic potential correlation

### Chart 3: Matrix Comparison (2×2 Grid of Bar Charts)
**Purpose:** Multi-dimensional regional comparison across 4 strategic metrics  
**Key Feature:** 
- Small multiples layout (4 independent bar charts)
- Metrics: Total GDP, GDP per Capita, Number of Countries, Total Population
- Same regional colors across all 4 charts
- Direct value labels on bars
**Design Rationale:** Enables "Region X vs. Y" comparison across multiple decision factors simultaneously

### Chart 4: Sunburst Chart
**Purpose:** Show hierarchical proportions in visually engaging format  
**Key Feature:** 
- Inner ring = Regions
- Outer ring = Countries (GDP < $100B grouped)
- Wedge size = GDP proportion
- Click-to-zoom interaction
**Design Rationale:** Complements treemap by emphasizing "part-of-whole" relationships instead of absolute sizes

### Chart 5: Top 10 Recommendations Table
**Purpose:** Translate data into actionable priorities  
**Key Feature:** 
- Ranked list with 7 columns (Rank, Country, Region, Total GDP, GDP per Capita, Population, Strategic Recommendation)
- Color-coded region badges
- Icon-based recommendation categories (🌟 Giant Market, 💎 Premium, 👥 Volume, etc.)
- Strategic Insights box below with 5 key takeaways
**Design Rationale:** Provides the "so what?" - clear actions Marco can assign to his team
