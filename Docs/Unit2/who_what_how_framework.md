## HOW: Data Strategy & Visualization Approach

### Core Data Framework
**3-Layer Analysis:**
1. **Regional Overview** → Strategic regional allocation
2. **Country Deep-Dive** → Tactical market entry priorities  
3. **Correlation Insights** → Product strategy validation

### Visualization Principles for Marco:

#### 1. Hierarchy First
- Use **TreeMaps** and **Sunburst Charts** to show nested relationships
- Region → Country → Economic metrics progression
- Size = Total GDP (purchasing power)
- Color = GDP per capita (premium market potential)

#### 2. Direct Data Labels
- Avoid cluttered axes; embed key numbers directly on visual elements
- Show absolute values ($B USD) and percentages simultaneously
- Highlight Top 10 countries with contrasting colors

#### 3. Horizontal Bar Charts (Not Pie!)
- For regional comparisons and country rankings
- Enable quick scanning and comparison
- Sort by descending value for instant prioritization

#### 4. Correlation Matrices → Business Translation
- Convert statistical correlations into strategic implications
- Population × Total GDP = **Consumer Base Strength**
- GDP per capita × Total GDP = **Premium Product Viability**
- Present as actionable segments: "High Volume/Low Margin" vs. "Low Volume/High Margin"

### Key Metrics to Emphasize:

**Primary Metrics:**
- Total GDP (B USD) → Market size & distribution scale potential
- GDP per capita (USD) → Product positioning & pricing power
- Population (M) → Labor force availability & consumer base

**Derived Metrics:**
- Market Concentration: Top 10 countries = 69.2% of global GDP
- Regional Balance: America vs. Asia (nearly equal at ~$38T vs. $37T)
- Economic Potential Score = Total GDP × 0.6 + (GDP per capita/1000) × 0.4

### Narrative Flow (3-Minute Structure):

**Minute 1: The Global Landscape (30 seconds)**
- 5 regions, 175 countries analyzed
- $107T global GDP, 7.7B population
- Market concentration insight: 69.2% in Top 10

**Minute 2: The Strategic Tri-Region Focus (90 seconds)**
- America, Asia, Europa = 96% of target GDP
- Show regional treemap with top countries highlighted
- Present correlation findings: Population = consumer base strength
- Key insight: Latin America & Asia offer best expansion balance

**Minute 3: The Action Plan (60 seconds)**
- Top 10 country priorities with 3-tier categorization:
  - **Tier 1:** Giant Markets (USA, China, Germany, Japan, India)
  - **Tier 2:** High Premium Potential (UK, France, Italy, Canada)
  - **Tier 3:** Volume Growth Plays (Brazil, Mexico, Indonesia)
- Product strategy: Mass-market focus for Asia/LatAm, premium for established Europa
- Next steps: Infrastructure assessment & geopolitical risk analysis
